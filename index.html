DZ Studio Labs Number Drop — Privacy Policy Last updated: October 23, 2026 This Privacy Policy describes how DZ Studio Labs ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use Number Drop (the "App"). Number Drop is a number-merging puzzle game available on mobile devices. 1. Information Stored on Your Device Number Drop stores certain game information locally on your device to support its features. This may include saved game progress, high scores, game statistics, language selection, theme, sound, and vibration preferences. These values are stored using local device storage. We do not operate a game account system or intentionally transmit this locally saved game information to our own servers. 2. Advertising and Third-Party Data Processing The App uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic information, subject to their policies, your device settings, and applicable law. Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on consent, settings, and availability. For details about Google's data practices, please see: Google Privacy Policy How Google uses information for advertising 3. Consent and Your Choices Where required, the App may request your consent for advertising-related data processing through Google's consent management tools. You can accept or decline available consent choices. You may also manage advertising and privacy settings through your device's operating system. On iOS, where applicable, the App may request permission for tracking under Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. We aim to honor applicable consent requirements before requesting advertisements. The availability and content of consent choices can vary by region and device. 4. Rewarded Advertisements Number Drop may offer optional rewarded advertisements that allow you to continue a game or receive an in-game benefit after watching an advertisement. Watching a rewarded advertisement is voluntary. 5. Data Sharing We do not sell your locally stored game progress or statistics. Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners to provide, measure, protect, and improve advertisements, in accordance with their policies and applicable law. 6. Data Retention and Deletion Game preferences and saved progress generally remain on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App, subject to device backup and restore behavior. Data processed by Google AdMob is governed by Google's applicable retention policies. For information about Google-related privacy controls, please refer to Google's privacy resources linked above. 7. Children's Privacy Number Drop is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through a registration or account feature. Advertising-related processing is subject to the relevant platform, advertising provider, and applicable age-related requirements. 8. Security We take reasonable steps to limit the information handled directly by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. 9.
